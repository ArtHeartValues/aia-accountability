A.i.A. — Accountability in AI Interaction Systems

A.i.A. is a detection and review system designed to surface ambiguity, verify interpretation, and make correction visible before confident output becomes harm.

The system focuses on a core failure point in conversational AI: interpretive drift — when systems move from input to conclusion without sufficiently surfacing uncertainty, ambiguity, or competing meanings.

What problem this addresses

Harm often emerges before the obvious violation

In AI-mediated systems, harm does not only come from explicit policy violations. It also emerges through ambiguity, unverified interpretation, overconfident framing, and silent normalization of uncertainty.

A.i.A. is designed to intervene earlier — at the level where meaning branches, confidence exceeds evidence, or correction would otherwise disappear into polished output.

This work is relevant to Trust & Safety, AI safety, and human risk analysis because it creates structured pathways for surfacing ambiguity, preserving user authorship, and recording what changed, why it changed, and whether interpretation was confirmed.

System workflow

From inquiry to accountable record

Inquiry — preserve raw input and assess applicability before interpretation.
Interpretation — identify branching meanings, glyph instability, tone shifts, and semantic ambiguity.
Verification — distinguish grounded claims, interpretation, uncertainty, and persuasion risk.
Record — make interventions and corrections visible, traceable, and reviewable.
Escalation — severity-tiered pathways (moderate / high / severe) with structured event classification.
Sanctuary — support clarity without replacing judgment, care, or agency.
Framework — keep the system charter, accountability spine, and protection standards visible.
Core signals

What the system is built to notice

Ambiguity

Detect when multiple materially different readings remain plausible and should not be silently collapsed into one.

Interpretive drift

Surface when systems move beyond what the input supports and begin acting on assumption rather than confirmed meaning.

Visible correction

Preserve a traceable record of intervention: what changed, why it changed, and whether the user confirmed alignment.
